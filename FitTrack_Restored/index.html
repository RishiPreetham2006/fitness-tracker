Frequently asked questions

Is FitTrack free to use?

Yes. The demo includes a free plan. Premium access is a simulated upgrade and does not charge real money.

Is my fitness data saved?

This version stores demo data in your browser. It is not a cloud account and does not sync across devices.

Can I use FitTrack as medical advice?

No. Fitness metrics are for general awareness and should not replace professional medical advice.
